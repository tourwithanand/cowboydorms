Who we are

A calm basecamp near Cochin Airport.

Cowboy Dorms is a backpackers dormitory located in Neduvannoor, Aluva, around 4 km from Cochin International Airport (COK). We offer an affordable and comfortable place to rest before or after a flight.

Our accommodation is designed for solo travellers, backpackers, transit passengers and groups looking for a practical stay close to the airport. Guests can relax in air-conditioned dorms and make use of facilities such as personal lockers, free Wi-Fi and an open kitchen.

Whether you are arriving late, catching an early flight or travelling through Kochi, Cowboy Dorms offers a convenient base in a quiet residential neighbourhood.
