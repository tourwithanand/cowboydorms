Rest well between journeys

A practical stay for airport travellers.

Cowboy Dorms offers AC dormitory accommodation in Neduvannoor, Aluva, around 4 km from Cochin International Airport (COK). The shared-dorm setup is suited to solo travellers, backpackers, transit passengers and groups.

Guests have access to air-conditioned dorm spaces, personal lockers, Wi-Fi, shared bathrooms and an open kitchen. The aim is to provide the essentials for a comfortable short stay close to the airport.
