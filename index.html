Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you arrive.

How far are you from Cochin International Airport (COK)?

We are located just 4 kilometers from Cochin International Airport. It typically takes less than 15 minutes by taxi or auto-rickshaw to reach our property.

Do you offer late-night or early-morning airport transfers?

Yes, absolutely. We understand international flights arrive at all hours. We can arrange reliable, safe 24/7 airport pickups and drop-offs. Please contact us via WhatsApp in advance to book your ride.

Is it safe for solo female backpackers & international travelers?

Security is our top priority. Our dormitory is situated in a highly peaceful and safe residential neighborhood in Neduvannoor. The property features secure lockers for your belongings, well-lit surroundings, and attentive staff to ensure a worry-free stay.

Do the dorms have Air Conditioning (AC) and Wi-Fi?

Yes! All our dormitories are fully air-conditioned to keep you comfortable in the tropical Kerala weather, and we offer high-speed, free Wi-Fi throughout the property so you can stay connected or work remotely.

Are there food options nearby?

You can use our open kitchen to prepare your own meals. If you prefer to eat out, there are local eateries nearby, and delivery apps like Swiggy and Zomato deliver food straight to the hostel door.
