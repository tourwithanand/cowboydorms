A Peaceful Transit Basecamp

Located just 4km from Cochin International Airport (COK), Cowboy Dorms is nestled in a calm, quiet, and nature-friendly residential area. We offer a soundless, peaceful environment perfect for resting, while remaining highly accessible.

Enjoy exceptionally neat, hygienic, fully air-conditioned dormitories equipped with high-speed Wi-Fi, ample vehicle parking, an open kitchen, and a peaceful rooftop. Our pleasant, professional, and multilingual staff are always on hand to ensure a welcoming and comfortable stay.
