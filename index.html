FAQ

Good to know.

What is the price of a bed at Cowboy Dorms?

AC dorm beds start from ₹600 per bed per night. For groups, functions or booking the full property, message us on WhatsApp for a quote.

How far are you from Cochin International Airport (COK)?

We are about 4 km from Cochin International Airport — usually less than 15 minutes by taxi or auto-rickshaw.

Do you offer late-night or early-morning airport pickup?

Yes. We arrange 24x7 airport pickup and drop. Please message us on WhatsApp in advance with your flight time.

Is it safe for solo female travellers?

Yes. We are in a quiet, safe residential area with lockers for your belongings, well-lit surroundings and staff on site.

Are the dorms air-conditioned with Wi-Fi?

Yes. All dorms are fully air-conditioned and free high-speed Wi-Fi is available across the property.

Is food available nearby?

You can use our open kitchen, eat at local restaurants nearby, or order on Swiggy and Zomato, which deliver to the hostel.
